# Timeback Machine™ Playbook (for AI assistants other than Claude)

This is the lighter version of the Timeback Machine™. It has no page and no daily check. Once a week, paste everything between the two lines into a new chat with your assistant. If your assistant can see your past chats, it uses them. If it cannot, paste in the titles and a line or two from your recent chats, or attach the files you worked on, before you send.

Keep the document it gives you. Next week, attach last week's document so it can carry your topics forward and leave out anything you said Never to.

---

You are running the Timeback Machine™ for me. Your job is to organize my recent work and suggest ways to give me time back. You only suggest. Do not change anything.

Look at my chats and files from the last 7 days (the last 90 days if this is the first time), plus last week's Timeback Machine™ document if I attached one. Then give me one document with these four sections:

1. **My topics.** The subjects I keep coming back to, each with a short plain name, one sentence on what it covers, and a list of the chats and files that belong to it, with dates. Put anything that fits nowhere under Unsorted. Use no more than 12 topics.

2. **Suggested hubs.** A subject qualifies when it came up in 3 or more chats in the last 60 days, or in 2 or more files, and I am deciding or building something about it, not only reading. For each, give a one-page summary: what I have decided so far, the facts I am working with, open questions, and a dated list of the sources. Leave out any subject I said Never to last week.

3. **Time I could get back.** Work I keep repeating: the same question answered again, the same document rebuilt, the same calculation redone, the same steps copied between tools. Keep only patterns that save me at least an hour a week, or that I clearly find painful. For each, give: what the fix is, how it would work, a cautious estimate of hours saved per week, the evidence (how many times and where), and the exact text I would paste into my assistant to set it up. No more than 3, best first.

4. **Can be archived.** Copies, older versions of something newer, and one-day items that have passed, each with the newer item that replaces it.

End with one line asking me to reply Yes, Not now or Never to each suggestion.

Rules: state only what my chats and files show. Never invent dates or counts. Skip personal, medical, financial account and family matters. Describe work, never people, and never report when or how long I worked. Write in plain sentences.
