Topics Hub with Hour Finder™

Feeling disorganized and overwhelmed with everything you do in Claude?

Your Claude chats and files pile up fast. Topics Hub scans your Claude chats and everything you've made with Claude, as far back as you choose, and organizes them under the subjects you keep coming back to, so you can find anything quickly.

Every day, Hour Finder™ looks at your new work, suggests a hub when a subject keeps coming up, and suggests ways to automate work you keep repeating, with an estimate of the hours it would give back.

Nothing happens until you approve it.

What it does for you

Two parts that work together. Topics Hub organizes everything you have done. Hour Finder™ keeps it organized every day and finds time to give back.

Topics Hub

Find anything in seconds

Every artifact, and every chat that belongs to a subject, becomes a card under the topics it belongs to. Stop scrolling through months of chat titles to find the one where you worked something out. Open the topic and it is there.

Topics Hub

One page for each subject that matters

When a subject keeps coming up, such as pricing your services, a home remodel or a job search, its hub pulls together your decisions, the facts you are working with, the questions still open and a link back to every source. You see where you landed without rereading old chats.

Topics Hub

Ask your own work

Type a question in the search box and Claude answers from your own topics and hubs. Answer an open question on a hub and it moves into your decisions on the next update.

Hour Finder™

Organized every day, without asking

Every day at the time you choose, Hour Finder™ files your new work, keeps each hub current and brings you fresh suggestions. You never have to ask Claude to tidy up again.

Hour Finder™

Hours back every week

When you keep repeating the same work, Hour Finder™ suggests a fix, such as a scheduled task, a reusable skill or a small app. Each suggestion estimates the hours a week it gives back and shows the chats that prove it. Your page keeps a running total.

Topics Hub + Hour Finder™

You stay in charge

Every suggestion waits for Yes, Not now or Never, and Never means it stops asking. Nothing is deleted. It reads only the sources you allow, skips personal matters, and your pages stay private until you share them.

Hour Finder™ + Prior Inventions

Your own inventions, captured as you go

When something in your work looks like a method, a piece of software or a design you came up with yourself, Hour Finder™ suggests adding it to your Prior Inventions page, already sorted under the type it fits, such as Methodologies and Frameworks, Software, or Designed but not built. Each suggestion shows why it fits and links to the work behind it. You can change the type before you say yes, and nothing is added until you approve it on your Prior Inventions page. Works with the free Prior Inventions page from Doovli.

Install it in Claude

You need a paid Claude plan. You choose how far back the first look goes: the last 90 days, 6 months, a year, or everything. For most people the 90-day look takes about ten minutes. The further back you go and the more you have in Claude, the longer it takes, because Claude reads through your chats and files one by one. You can leave the tab open and come back. When it finishes, everything is organized, and it stays that way.

What happens when you click

  1. Click the button. It copies a short install message and opens a new Claude chat.
  2. In the new chat, paste the message (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac) and press Enter.
  3. Click your answers to Claude's five quick questions: your name, your time zone, when the daily check should run, how far back the first look should go, and which sources it may read.
  4. When Claude asks permission to set up your page or the daily check, click Allow.
  5. Your Topics Hub is pinned in your Claude sidebar. For one-click access from your desktop, click Add to my desktop at the top right of your Topics Hub, then Install.

Any AI assistant

Use ChatGPT, Gemini or another assistant?

The full version needs Claude, because it uses pages that save your data, a daily check and search across your past chats. For any other assistant there is a lighter version: the Hour Finder™ Playbook. Once a week, paste it into a new chat. Your assistant gives you back one document with your topics, suggested hubs, the time you could get back, and what can be archived. There is no page and no automatic daily check, so next week you attach last week's document to carry your topics forward.
