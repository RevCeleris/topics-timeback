Topics Hub with Timeback Machine™

Feeling disorganized and overwhelmed with everything you do in Claude?

Your Claude chats and files pile up fast. Topics Hub files them under the subjects you keep coming back to, so you can find anything again.

Every day, Timeback Machine™ looks at your new work, suggests a hub when a subject keeps coming up, and suggests ways to automate work you keep repeating, with an estimate of the hours it would give back.

Nothing happens until you approve it.

What it does for you

Two parts that work together. Topics Hub organizes everything you have done. Timeback Machine™ keeps it organized every day and finds time to give back.

Topics Hub

Find anything in seconds

Every artifact, and every chat that belongs to a subject, becomes a card under the topics it belongs to. Stop scrolling through months of chat titles to find the one where you worked something out. Open the topic and it is there.

Topics Hub

One page for each subject that matters

When a subject keeps coming up, such as pricing your services, a home remodel or a job search, its hub pulls together your decisions, the facts you are working with, the questions still open and a link back to every source. You see where you landed without rereading old chats.

Topics Hub

Ask your own work

Type a question in the search box and Claude answers from your own topics and hubs. Answer an open question on a hub and it moves into your decisions on the next update.

Timeback Machine™

Organized every day, without asking

Every day at the time you choose, Timeback Machine™ files your new work, keeps each hub current and brings you fresh suggestions. You never have to ask Claude to tidy up again.

Timeback Machine™

Hours back every week

When you keep repeating the same work, Timeback Machine™ suggests a fix, such as a scheduled task, a reusable skill or a small app. Each suggestion estimates the hours a week it gives back and shows the chats that prove it. Your page keeps a running total.

Topics Hub + Timeback Machine™

You stay in charge

Every suggestion waits for Yes, Not now or Never, and Never means it stops asking. Nothing is deleted. It reads only the sources you allow, skips personal matters, and your pages stay private until you share them.

Install it in Claude

You need a paid Claude plan. For most people setup takes about ten minutes. If you have a lot in your Claude account, the first look takes longer, because Claude reads through your last 90 days of chats and files one by one. You can leave the tab open and come back. When it finishes, everything is organized, and it stays that way.

Copy the message and open Claude

  1. Click the button. It copies a short install message and opens a new Claude chat.
  2. In the new chat, paste the message (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac) and press Enter.
  3. Click your answers to Claude's four quick questions: your name, your time zone, when the daily check should run, and which sources it may read.
  4. When Claude asks permission to set up your page or the daily check, click Allow.

If pasting does not work, copy the message from the box below. Prefer to set it up by hand? Download the manual install.
